Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

How each palette contributes to the vibes and usage across the web and the reasoning behind each choice.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: For the complementary palette, I started with the bright green. I do love a good green, but I decided to switch it up and make it's complement, the pink, the primary color. From there, I lightened the green almost to white. I chose a couple of colors from between the pink and green to create a balanced and visually appealing palette.

Mood: The vibrant, watermelon-esque feel of this palette gives it a fun and summery vibe.

Website Usage: I do feel like this palette has potential for possibly a quirky cosmetics or skin-care brand. It would need some adjustments as the green on pink might be too harsh for some users. And any red-green palette carries with it the potential for accessibility issues.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: The primary color on this palette is one of my favorite deep forest greens. Splitting out from there to either side, are two more rich hues. I then chose two colors splitting the difference between the primary and the other two, and lightened one to create a harmonious and visually pleasing palette.

Mood: The rooted, earthy feel of this palette gives a sense of depth, stability, connection, and calm.

Website Usage: This palette could be used for many avenues, as it's not overly saturated or overwhelming. It would work well for a nature-themed website or a brand that wants to convey a sense of tranquility and connection like a spa or a wellness center.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I started with a vibrant mustard yellow. Spacing the colors evenly around the color wheel gave me a beautiful pink and blue. Since the three colors are equidistant from each other, there isn't one that stands out more than the others, so I selected the blue as the accent. I then lightened and muted the yellow, and darkened and muted the pink to create that balanced feel.

Mood: Since the colors cover a large chunk of the color wheel, the feeling is very dynamic and energetic.

Website Usage: I feel like this is another one that could use a little tweaking to make it less overwhelming in areas. I could see this working for a creative or artistic brand or a brand that appeals to a younger demographic.
