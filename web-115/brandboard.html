Brand Board

McRoffis Designs

Deep-Rooted Design

McRoffis Designs is a small business that provides web design and development services. The brand is focused on creating visually appealing and user-friendly websites for clients in various industries. The brand's aesthetic is centered around a natural, earthy color palette and clean, modern typography. This brand board serves as a reference for the visual identity of the company.

Playfair Display

Playfair Display is an elegant serif font that is perfect for creating a sophisticated look and feel. It will be used for titles, headings, and subheadings.

Raleway is a clean and modern sans-serif font that complements Playfair Display. It will be used for body text, captions, and navigation elements.
